# March 2026 — Board Report

---

## Overview

### How did the company do overall?

March confirmed the spring season with **$348,020 in gross revenue** — up **14.1% YoY** vs $305,054 in March 2025, and up **85% vs February 2026** ($187,594). While revenue came in below the $433,213 budget (-19.7%), the business delivered strong unit economics with **69.5% gross margin**, **5.04x MER**, and **22.5% CM2**. The month-over-month trajectory is strong: daily revenue averaged $15.1K with consistent $13-19K days throughout the month.

### Wins
- **Revenue growth vs prior year** — +14.1% YoY marks the first month in 2026 to exceed LY, recovering from Jan (-27%) and Feb (-32%)
- **Ad spend discipline** — $68,993 actual vs $72,602 budget, $3.6K under budget while delivering 14% YoY growth
- **MER improvement** — 5.04x (March) recovered from 4.11x (February), approaching January's 5.88x
- **CM2 turned decisively positive** at $78,186 (22.5%) vs February's -$11,900 (-6.3%)
- **Amazon channel growing** — $17,870 revenue (284+ orders), up 67% vs February's $8,089
- **New customer acquisition efficient** — 2,106 new customers at $32.76 CAC
- **Gross margin outperforming budget** at 69.5% (budget was ~72% for the month)

### Losses
- **Revenue missed budget by $85K (-19.7%)** — the delayed 2026 spring start cost us approximately 2 weeks of peak-season revenue in Q1
- **Shipping costs remain elevated** — $90,437 paid vs $12,080 collected ($78K net shipping cost)
- **YTD still trailing LY** — $619,686 vs $696,017 LY (-11.0%) and vs $825,509 budget (-24.9%)
- **AOV compression** — $126.23 vs ~$165 in February, driven by higher Amazon mix (lower AOV) and more small DTC orders

### Exciting News
- **Shipping Insights dashboard launched** — real-time carrier cost tracking, per-pound analysis, and collected-vs-paid visibility
- **FBA inventory tracking separated** from local warehouse — dedicated forecasting for Amazon channel
- **Order fulfillment monitoring live** — aging buckets (<1d, <5d, >10d) now tracked on dashboard
- **Google Ads optimization automated** — Animal Broad campaign restructured, zero-impression keywords cleaned, budget reallocated
- **Product reviews feed fixed** — 1,053 reviews now flowing to Google Merchant Center with proper GTIN/MPN matching
- **February actuals integrated** — P&L now uses real fixed costs for completed months, budget for projections
