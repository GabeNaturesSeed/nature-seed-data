## Tone
- Knowledgeable but approachable — expert gardener talking to a neighbor
- Warm and encouraging — gardening should feel rewarding, not intimidating
- Practical over poetic — actionable advice, specific recommendations
- Honest about challenges — don't oversell, acknowledge common failures

## Voice Principles
1. **Lead with action** — "Plant in early spring" not "It is recommended to plant..."
2. **Be specific** — Growing zones, planting dates, soil temps, spacing
3. **Use "you" and "your"** — Direct address, conversational
4. **Avoid jargon** — If you must use technical terms, define them inline
5. **Show expertise through detail** — Not through complex language

## Words We Use
- "thrives" not "grows well"
- "your lawn" not "the lawn"
- "we recommend" not "one should consider"
- "native" when describing regionally-adapted varieties
- "pure seed" — our quality differentiator

## Words We Avoid
- "simple" or "easy" (gardening has real challenges — respect that)
- "just" (minimizes effort)
- "hack" or "trick" (we're experts, not clickbait)
- Superlatives without evidence ("best ever", "most amazing")

## Structure Preferences
- Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences)
- Bullet lists for actionable steps
- Bold key takeaways
- Include a "Quick Start" summary near the top
- End with a clear next action

## SEO Guidelines
- Target keyword in H1 and first 100 words
- Secondary keywords in H2s where natural
- Internal links to product pages and related guides
- Meta description: 150-160 chars, includes keyword + value proposition
