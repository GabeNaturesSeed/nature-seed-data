# Nature's Seed Image Style Guide

## Photography Style
- Natural lighting — golden hour or soft overcast preferred
- Earthy color palette — greens, browns, warm golds
- Real hands in soil — show the human connection to planting
- Close-up detail shots of seeds, seedlings, soil texture
- Wide establishing shots of lush gardens, lawns, meadows

## AI Image Generation Prompts Should Include
- "natural lighting, soft shadows"
- "earthy tones, green and warm gold color palette"
- "photorealistic, editorial quality"
- "gardening, organic, sustainable"
- "shallow depth of field for detail shots"

## AI Image Generation Prompts Should Avoid
- Artificial/studio lighting
- Oversaturated colors
- Stock photo aesthetic (forced smiles, staged scenes)
- Text overlays or watermarks
- Abstract or illustration styles (unless specifically requested)

## Image Dimensions
- Hero: 1600x900 (16:9)
- In-article: 1200x800 (3:2)
- Product: 1200x1200 (1:1)
- Thumbnail: 600x400 (3:2)

## Alt Text Guidelines
- Describe what's IN the image, not the article topic
- Include plant names when visible
- Mention action ("hands planting tomato seedlings in raised bed")
- Keep under 125 characters
- Don't start with "Image of" or "Photo of"
