### Confidence Notes

- **High confidence**: Walmart revenue (directly measurable, new channel)
- **Medium confidence**: Email attribution (GA4 last-click model), Google Ads (conversion tracking), Seasonal baseline (based on single YoY comparison)
- **Low confidence**: Theme CVR impact (confounded by traffic mix), Texas Collection (requires SKU-level analysis)
- **Interaction effects**: Many improvements happened simultaneously. Email campaigns drive paid search brand queries. Theme improvements help ALL channels convert better. True isolated impact is unknowable without A/B testing.

### Recommendations

1. **Continue Walmart investment** — even at current run rate, it adds meaningful incremental revenue at zero ad cost
2. **Maintain email volume** — the lift from Klaviyo campaigns is real and measurable
3. **Monitor CVR closely** — if CVR improves as seasonal traffic normalizes in April, that confirms theme value
4. **Build Texas Collection tracking** — tag TX products with UTM/SKU prefixes to isolate their contribution
5. **Run this analysis monthly** — the seasonal baseline will sharpen with more data points
