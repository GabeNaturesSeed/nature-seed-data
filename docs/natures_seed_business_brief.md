## Who We Are

Nature's Seed (The NativeSeed Group) is a vertically-integrated seed company based in Lehi, Utah. We grow seed on our own U.S. farms, collect wildland seed across 13 western states, and partner with producers across the U.S. and Canada. Our team includes seed scientists, farmers, and conservationists.

We sell direct-to-consumer through naturesseed.com and on Amazon and Walmart marketplaces. We're exploring wholesale retail through Menards (300+ Midwest stores).

**Founded:** Family-owned, operating since the mid-1990s.
**Headquarters:** Lehi, UT 84043

- **Vertically integrated** — We grow on our own farms (cost control + quality assurance)
- **Regionally tailored blends** — Not one-size-fits-all commodity seed
- **Multi-channel** — DTC + Amazon + Walmart + wholesale (most competitors are single-channel)
- **Automation-first operations** — AI-driven marketing, reporting, and optimization at near-zero infrastructure cost
- **Expert support** — Seed scientists available to help customers select the right product

### Market Trends
- Growing consumer interest in **native plants, pollinator gardens, and low-maintenance lawns** (clover, micro clover)
- **Pasture and livestock** segment steady — driven by hobby farms and rural property owners
- **Sustainability** messaging resonating — non-GMO, filler-free, farm-direct
- **Amazon marketplace** growing as a discovery channel for specialty seed (lower AOV but new customer acquisition)
