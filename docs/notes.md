## What Drove the March Growth

March surged to $513K — up 173% from February's $188K and 14.2% above March 2025's $450K. Growth came from both operational improvements and the seasonal ramp arriving.

| Growth Driver | Impact | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Spring demand (seasonality)** | Primary driver | Late Feb to Mar ramp consistent with 2025 pattern, delayed ~2 weeks |
| **Scaled Google Ads profitably** | $102K spend at 4.85x MER | Increased budget while maintaining efficiency across Shopping and Broad campaigns |
| **New website (launched Q1)** | Conversion rate multiplier | Higher-converting site compounds every dollar of traffic spend |
| **Amazon scaling organically** | +$23K MoM | $31K with zero incremental ad spend — channel building its own momentum |
| **Walmart (new channel)** | $7.9K incremental | Second full month, zero advertising cost, 100% additive revenue |
| **Email marketing program** | Engagement + repeat purchases | 55 campaigns deployed across March-May to sustain customer lifecycle |

**The compounding effect is clear.** Higher ad budget on a better-converting site, combined with two marketplace channels that did not exist last year, delivered the best March in company history.

## Initiatives (March-April)

1. **Ad campaign optimization** — Keyword coverage expanded 125% (67 to 150 keywords), improving impression share and reach across Google Shopping and Search
2. **Product review syndication to Google Shopping** — 1,053 verified reviews now display as star ratings on Shopping ads, improving click-through rates
3. **Amazon FBA inventory separation + demand forecasting** — Ensures stock availability as the channel scales toward $30K+/month
4. **Menards wholesale catalog** — 77 SKUs with validated delivered pricing, ready for buyer submission across 300+ store locations
5. **Post-purchase review collection** — Automated flow targeting all 2026 customers, including a backfill campaign for customers who were never asked
6. **Finance actuals integration** — Dashboard now uses real P&L data for closed months, improving reporting accuracy for board and management
7. **Shipping cost analysis** — Carrier-level cost breakdown (per-pound, collected vs paid) to identify margin improvement opportunities
